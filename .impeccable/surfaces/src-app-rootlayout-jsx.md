# Storefront (all routes)

Scope: whole storefront, Persuade on home, Operate on shop / product / bag / checkout / account.
Audience: men shopping for an occasion, mostly on phones. Job: find the right piece, size it, buy before the date.
Constraints: user-pinned references (white editorial menswear grid + three-photo hero), logo bronze + black, feature parity with Ekatra.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A designer-label lookbook that happens to sell. Photography in tall 2:3 frames carries every page; the interface is a thin black and bronze frame around it. Refuses the marketplace default of busy banners, rounded cards and coloured badges everywhere.

OWN-WORLD: Clean warm-white ground (#fcfbf9) with stone bands (#efede8), true off-black ink (#151413) for text and primary buttons, burnished bronze (#9b6428) sampled from the logo for the sunburst ornament, sale tags, active states and focus. Zero corner radius everywhere. Bodoni Moda (echoes the logo's high-contrast serif) for large display lines only; Tenor Sans uppercase with wide tracking for titles, nav and buttons; Albert Sans for body and UI. The logo's sunburst is the single ornament.

STORY: Visitor sees the season's three looks, understands it is occasion menswear, picks a category or occasion, opens a piece, chooses size and add-on, buys.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Centered logo header with search left and icons right, nav row under it. Below, a three-photo triptych across the full content width, centre frame taller and wider; a short Bodoni headline and one "Shop the edit" button sit under the centre frame. The three frames change together as a set every few seconds, each revealing with a vertical clip wipe staggered left, centre, right.

FORM: Editorial lookbook storefront, position 1 of the user-pinned options; seed key: user-pinned (no roll). Signature interaction: the staggered clip-wipe triptych; motion grammar is clip-path reveals and quiet fades with a strong ease-out, nothing bouncy.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
